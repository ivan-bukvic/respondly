# Safety & Contraindications — Lumin Aesthetic Clinic

## Pregnancy & Breastfeeding

Botox, dermal fillers, chemical peels, laser hair removal, and body contouring are not performed on clients who are pregnant or breastfeeding, as safety for these treatments has not been established for these groups. HydraFacial (without certain active boosters) may be considered safe — this must be escalated to a licensed provider for individual review before booking.

## Who Should Not Receive Botox or Filler

Clients with a known allergy to any ingredient in Botox, Dysport, Xeomin, or filler products should not receive these treatments. Clients with certain neuromuscular conditions (such as myasthenia gravis or Eaton-Lambert syndrome) are not candidates for neuromodulator treatment. Any client with an active skin infection or inflammation in the treatment area must postpone treatment until fully healed.

## Medication Interactions

Certain medications and supplements increase the risk of bruising or bleeding during injectable treatments, including blood thinners (such as warfarin or aspirin), certain antibiotics, and high-dose fish oil or vitamin E supplements. Clients on any prescription blood thinner must have their treatment plan reviewed and escalated to a licensed provider before any injectable procedure is scheduled.

## Allergic Reactions

If you have a known allergy to lidocaine, hyaluronic acid, or any injectable ingredient, please disclose this during your consultation. Signs of an allergic reaction can include hives, significant swelling beyond the treatment area, difficulty breathing, or dizziness. Any of these symptoms following treatment require immediate medical attention — this is not something we can advise on remotely and must be escalated to a licensed provider or emergency care immediately.

## Skin Conditions & Laser Treatments

Clients with active cold sores, eczema flare-ups, psoriasis, or open wounds in the treatment area should not proceed with laser hair removal, chemical peels, or microneedling until the area has fully healed. Clients with a history of keloid scarring should discuss microneedling and laser treatments with a provider before booking, as these treatments carry additional risk for this group.

## Isotretinoin (Accutane) Use

Clients who have used isotretinoin (commonly known as Accutane) within the past 6 months should not receive chemical peels, microneedling, or laser hair removal due to increased risk of scarring and skin sensitivity. Please disclose recent isotretinoin use during your consultation.

## When to Seek Immediate Medical Attention

While rare, certain symptoms after any treatment require urgent attention rather than a routine follow-up message: sudden vision changes or eye pain after facial injections, signs of skin necrosis (blanching or a dusky/purple discoloration at the injection site), difficulty breathing or swallowing, or any symptom that feels severe or rapidly worsening. In any of these cases, clients should seek emergency medical care immediately and contact the clinic as soon as possible afterward. These situations are always escalated to a licensed provider and are never handled through automated responses alone.

## General Disclosure Requirement

All clients are required to disclose their full medical history, current medications, and any prior adverse reactions to cosmetic treatments during their initial consultation. This information directly affects which treatments can be safely offered.
