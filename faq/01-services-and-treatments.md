# Services & Treatments — Lumin Aesthetic Clinic

## Botox & Anti-Wrinkle Injections

Lumin offers FDA-approved neuromodulator treatments (Botox, Dysport, and Xeomin) to soften dynamic wrinkles, including forehead lines, crow's feet, and frown lines between the brows. Treatments are performed by our licensed injectors and typically take 15-20 minutes. Results begin appearing within 3-5 days and reach full effect at around two weeks. Effects generally last 3-4 months, after which a touch-up session is recommended.

## Dermal Fillers

We offer hyaluronic acid-based dermal fillers (including Juvederm and Restylane lines) to restore volume, smooth lines, and enhance facial contours. Common treatment areas include lips, cheeks, nasolabial folds, and under-eye hollows. A typical session lasts 30-45 minutes. Results are immediate and generally last 6-18 months depending on the product and treatment area.

## Chemical Peels

Lumin offers a range of chemical peels from light (lunchtime peels with no downtime) to medium-depth peels for more pronounced texture and pigmentation concerns. Peels can help with acne scarring, sun damage, fine lines, and uneven skin tone. Our estheticians will recommend a peel strength based on a skin consultation.

## Laser Hair Removal

We use medical-grade diode laser technology for permanent hair reduction on all skin tones. Common treatment areas include underarms, legs, bikini line, and face. Most clients require 6-8 sessions spaced 4-6 weeks apart for optimal results. A complimentary patch test is required before the first session for new clients.

## HydraFacial

A non-invasive treatment combining cleansing, exfoliation, extraction, and hydration in one session. Suitable for nearly all skin types with no downtime. Many clients book HydraFacial monthly as part of an ongoing skincare routine.

## Microneedling

Microneedling uses fine sterile needles to stimulate the skin's natural collagen production, improving texture, fine lines, and acne scarring. We also offer microneedling with PRP (platelet-rich plasma) for enhanced results. A typical course is 3-4 sessions spaced 4-6 weeks apart.

## Body Contouring

Lumin offers non-invasive body contouring using cryolipolysis (fat-freezing) technology to reduce stubborn fat pockets in areas such as the abdomen, flanks, and thighs. This is not a weight-loss treatment but a body-shaping treatment for clients close to their goal weight. Results develop gradually over 8-12 weeks following treatment.

## Initial Consultations

All new clients — regardless of the treatment they are interested in — are required to complete an initial consultation with one of our licensed providers before any treatment is scheduled. Consultations allow us to review medical history, discuss goals, and confirm that a treatment is appropriate and safe for the individual client.
