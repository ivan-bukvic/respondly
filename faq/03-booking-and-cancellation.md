# Booking & Cancellation Policy — Lumin Aesthetic Clinic

## How to Book an Appointment

Appointments can be requested via WhatsApp, phone, or through our website booking form. New clients must complete a brief intake form and schedule an initial consultation before their first treatment. Returning clients can book treatment appointments directly, subject to provider availability.

## Deposits

A non-refundable deposit of $50 is required to secure appointments for treatments over $200 (including Botox, filler, laser packages, and body contouring). The deposit is applied toward the total cost of your treatment on the day of your appointment.

## Cancellation Policy

We require at least 24 hours' notice to cancel or reschedule an appointment without penalty. Cancellations made with less than 24 hours' notice will forfeit their deposit (if applicable) or be charged a $50 cancellation fee.

## Rescheduling

Rescheduling requests made more than 24 hours before the appointment are free of charge and can be handled via WhatsApp or phone. Same-day rescheduling is treated the same as a late cancellation.

## Late Arrivals

Clients arriving more than 15 minutes late to their appointment may need to reschedule, as we may be unable to accommodate the full treatment time. Please message us on WhatsApp if you are running late so we can advise on next steps.

## No-Show Policy

A no-show (failure to attend without any notice) will result in forfeiture of the deposit and may require a deposit for all future bookings, regardless of treatment cost.

## Group & Multiple Treatment Bookings

Clients wishing to book multiple treatments in a single visit (for example, a HydraFacial followed by a peel) should mention this when booking so we can allocate sufficient appointment time. Some treatment combinations require provider approval and must be escalated to a licensed provider before confirming, as certain treatments cannot safely be performed on the same day.

## Membership & Package Cancellations

Monthly memberships (such as the HydraFacial membership) can be cancelled at any time with 7 days' notice before the next billing date. Prepaid treatment packages (such as laser hair removal packages) are non-refundable but do not expire.
