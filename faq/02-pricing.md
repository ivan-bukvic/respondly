# Pricing — Lumin Aesthetic Clinic

## Botox & Anti-Wrinkle Injections Pricing

Botox and Dysport are priced per unit, starting at $12/unit. Most forehead or frown-line treatments use between 20-40 units, typically totaling $240-$480 per session. Xeomin is priced at a flat rate of $325 for a standard treatment area. Exact pricing depends on the number of units required to achieve your goals, which will be discussed during your consultation.

## Dermal Filler Pricing

Dermal filler is priced per syringe, starting at $650/syringe. Most lip or cheek treatments require 1-2 syringes. Under-eye filler treatments start at $750/syringe due to the specialized technique required. A full price breakdown is provided during your consultation based on your treatment goals.

## Chemical Peel Pricing

- Light/lunchtime peel: $125 per session
- Medium-depth peel: $225 per session
- Package of 3 peels (any type): 10% discount when purchased together

## Laser Hair Removal Pricing

Pricing varies by treatment area:

- Underarms: $85/session or $425 for a package of 6
- Bikini line: $110/session or $550 for a package of 6
- Full legs: $250/session or $1,250 for a package of 6
- Face (upper lip or chin): $65/session or $325 for a package of 6

## HydraFacial Pricing

- Signature HydraFacial: $175 per session
- Deluxe HydraFacial (with added boosters): $225 per session
- Monthly membership (one treatment/month): $150/month, cancel anytime

## Microneedling Pricing

- Standard microneedling: $300 per session
- Microneedling with PRP: $500 per session
- Package of 3 sessions (standard): 10% discount when purchased together

## Body Contouring Pricing

Body contouring is priced per treatment cycle and varies based on the number of areas treated. A single area starts at $600 per cycle. Most clients require 2-3 cycles per area for optimal results. Multi-area packages are available at a discounted rate — ask during your consultation.

## Payment & Financing

We accept all major credit cards, debit cards, and HSA/FSA cards where applicable. Lumin also partners with CareCredit to offer financing options for treatment packages over $500. A deposit is required to secure most appointments — see our Booking & Cancellation Policy for details. We do not accept personal checks.
