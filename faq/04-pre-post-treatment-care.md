# Pre & Post-Treatment Care — Lumin Aesthetic Clinic

## Before Botox & Filler

Avoid alcohol for 24 hours before your appointment, as it can increase bruising risk. If medically appropriate and approved by your own physician, avoid blood-thinning medications and supplements (such as aspirin, ibuprofen, fish oil, and vitamin E) for one week prior. Arrive with a clean face, free of makeup, if possible.

## After Botox & Filler

Avoid touching, rubbing, or massaging the treated area for 24 hours. Stay upright and avoid lying down for 4 hours after treatment. Avoid strenuous exercise, alcohol, and excessive heat exposure (saunas, hot yoga, sunbathing) for 24 hours. Mild swelling, redness, or bruising at injection sites is normal and typically resolves within a few days.

## Before Chemical Peels

Discontinue use of retinoids, exfoliating acids, and other active skincare ingredients 3-5 days before your peel, unless otherwise directed by your provider. Avoid sun exposure and tanning in the days leading up to your appointment, as sun-damaged or sunburned skin cannot safely be treated.

## After Chemical Peels

Skin may appear red, tight, or begin to peel within 2-5 days depending on peel depth — this is a normal part of the process and should not be picked or forced. Use a gentle, fragrance-free cleanser and moisturizer, and apply broad-spectrum SPF 30+ daily, even on cloudy days. Avoid active skincare ingredients (retinoids, acids, scrubs) until peeling has fully resolved, generally 5-7 days.

## Before Laser Hair Removal

Shave the treatment area 24 hours before your appointment — do not wax, pluck, or use depilatory creams, as the hair follicle needs to be intact for the laser to work effectively. Avoid sun exposure and tanning (including self-tanner) for at least 2 weeks prior, as treating tanned or sunburned skin increases risk of side effects.

## After Laser Hair Removal

Mild redness and slight swelling around hair follicles is normal and typically subsides within a few hours. Avoid sun exposure and always apply SPF 30+ to treated areas for at least 2 weeks after treatment. Avoid hot showers, saunas, and strenuous exercise for 24-48 hours to minimize irritation.

## After Microneedling

Skin may appear red and feel similar to a mild sunburn for 24-48 hours. Avoid makeup for at least 24 hours post-treatment to reduce infection risk. Avoid active skincare ingredients, sun exposure, and swimming pools for 3-5 days. Always apply SPF 30+ once skin has calmed, generally after 48 hours.

## General Aftercare Tips

Regardless of treatment type, staying well-hydrated and avoiding alcohol and excessive sun exposure in the days following any procedure supports faster healing and better results. If you experience anything beyond mild, expected side effects — such as significant pain, signs of infection, or an allergic reaction — please contact us immediately or seek medical attention.
